## Short description

Bank the perfect shot and shatter a whole reactor. Chain explosions, split your volley, and master 30 precision ricochet puzzles.

## Full description

Ricochet Foundry is a satisfying aiming puzzle game where one clever angle can start a huge chain reaction. Launch a volley of energy balls, bounce around indestructible steel, and break every colored core before your shots run out.

Red overloads blast nearby blocks. Mint splitters multiply your volley. Find the right combination and turn a crowded reactor into a shower of sparks.

Work through 30 chambers across five sectors, collect up to 90 stars, and return for a new daily reactor. Take your time, rewind a volley, and try a better angle. No timers, energy limits, or paid upgrades.

## Controls text

Mouse: aim in the chamber and release the left button to fire. Touch: drag to aim, then lift your finger. Keyboard: Left/Right to aim, Space to fire, Z to rewind, R to restart, P to pause.

## Reviewer notes

- New players enter the first playable chamber directly. Tutorial is non-blocking and skippable. A straight center shot wins the first chamber.
- Basic Launch build has no advertisements or ad prompts.
- SDK v3 loads dynamically, initializes before SDK calls, and reports loading/gameplay state. Saves use Data when available and localStorage as fallback. Progress merges preserve previously earned stars.
- No collection of personal data by game code, no third-party tracking, no login requirement, no external promotions or custom fullscreen button.
- There is a mute control and a reduced-motion option. The game automatically suspends simulation/audio when hidden; iOS audio resumes on a valid user gesture.
- Daily personal best is local/cloud save data, **not an online leaderboard**. No network or server is needed for daily content.
- All game art and sound are generated by the supplied source code. Covers use the same original art style.
